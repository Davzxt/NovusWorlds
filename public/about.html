About Novus Worlds

Release: 2008

What is Novus Worlds?

Novus Worlds is a free online platform where you can create and play games. Inspired by the classic Roblox experience from 2008.

Features

  • Create 3D games with our Studio
  • Play thousands of user-created games
  • Customize your avatar with items from the catalog
  • Multiplayer real-time gaming
  • Virtual economy with Novux

Technology

Built with Node.js, Three.js, and WebSocket for real-time multiplayer.
